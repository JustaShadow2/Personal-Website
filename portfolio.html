AI Engineer Intern · Calgary

  • Served ACE-Step DiT in production (turbo / SFT / base plus a 0.6B lyric LM) with FP8 weight-only quantization and compiled kernels, targeting a finished 48 kHz stereo master in under 10 seconds on the Quick path and a 15s compose SLA — vs. typical multi-minute waits on closed music-gen APIs.
  • Built compose generation for 10–240s songs (4 min Turbo cap) across 50+ languages, with 16 BPM-conditioned genre presets, 5 lyric structure templates (instrumental through verse / pre-chorus / chorus / bridge / outro), CoT lyric writing when lyrics are omitted, and seed-reproducible multi-take output (take i = seed+i).
  • Engineered a CLAP best-of-N quality gate that generates n+overgenerate takes (default 2 kept + 1 discarded; up to 4 kept + 3 extra), reranks by CLAP, and returns winners with scores, LUFS, per-stage timings, and an sla_met flag — cutting weak takes before users ever hear them.
  • Added default-on ASR lyric verification (word-overlap bar ~0.20) that regenerates until n takes actually sing the prompt, with duration-scaled retry caps (12 / 6 / 6 for short / mid / long, max 16) so vocal tracks fail closed instead of shipping mismatched lyrics.
  • Shipped M2 editing: cover/remix, time-bounded section repaint (10-frame latent + 0.05s WAV crossfades, 2s context), worker extend, and multi-hop async orchestrated extend with 4s overlap stitching so tracks truly lengthen instead of turbo-repainting in place — non-destructive, with version history.
  • Upgraded stem separation to a live MelBand-RoFormer engine (Kim FT checkpoint) with BS-RoFormer secondary and Demucs fallback, exposing instrumental fetch plus a 6-stem Studio ZIP (vocals, drums, bass, synths, FX, master) for DAW handoff.
  • Designed ownership and trust into the product: per-track provenance (models, settings, exact seed), inaudible watermark, unlocked 320 kbps MP3 and 48 kHz WAV exports, prompts never used as training data, and an open-weight self-host / white-label path (99.9% uptime target on the hosted API) so teams can run inference on their own GPUs.
