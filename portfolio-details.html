Project

PokeQuant

Quantitative analytics for Pokemon TCG markets — fair value models, demand signals, grading ROI, and sealed-product intelligence.

What shipped

A full analytics surface for cards, sets, sealed product, and leaderboards — backed by versioned predictions and multi-source market history.

  • Multi-source price history across TCGPlayer market prices and eBay raw / PSA 9 / PSA 10 smart prices, plus volume views for TCGPlayer sales and 7-day PSA 10 eBay volume.
  • ML fair-value predictions per card with predicted price, 90% confidence interval, UNDERVALUED / FAIR VALUE / OVERVALUED signal, confidence tier, and ratio versus live market.
  • Demand scoring as a 0–10 composite from search interest and 14-day / 30-day price momentum, filterable and rankable in the UI.
  • PSA 10 grading ROI computed as smart graded price divided by raw eBay price, with confidence tied to sold-listing sample size.
  • Snapshot data model for prices, eBay graded metrics, sales, demand signals, sealed products, and versioned model_predictions to keep nightly refreshes reproducible.
  • Leaderboards ranking cards by momentum, PSA 10 ROI, and sales volume; sets by premium metrics and 30-day movement; sealed booster boxes, ETBs, and packs by price and change.
